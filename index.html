Our Story

Founded in 2023 in the heart of Addis Ababa, AK-Y House brings together the rich culinary traditions of Ethiopia with contemporary international cuisine.

Our chefs craft each dish with locally sourced ingredients, honoring traditional recipes while embracing modern techniques. From our signature Ethiopian coffee ceremony to our gourmet international selections, every visit is a journey of flavors.
